## Pantone

Not computed. Pantone matching is a licensed lookup against physical ink standards and cannot be derived from a hex value. Take the printed proof to a Pantone Formula Guide under D50 lighting and pick by eye, then record separate coated (C) and uncoated (U) references. The same ink looks materially different on the two stocks.
